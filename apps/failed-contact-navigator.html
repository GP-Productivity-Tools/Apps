Failed Contact Navigator

For triage requests where we've replied through Accurx and heard nothing back: no reply, no booking, nothing returned. Most of this is a notes review. You only phone when you're significantly concerned.

No patient-identifiable information. Nothing typed here is saved or sent anywhere, and it clears when you start a new case. Use the request reference, not a name or NHS number. This tool supports clinical judgement; it doesn't replace it.
Example case loaded so you can see how it works. Press Start new case for a real one.

02

Review the notes: does the silence matter?

Re-read the request and the record. The tier decides how long you can wait and whether you need to pick up the phone.

Is the patient potentially vulnerable, alone, or at risk of potentially life-threatening symptoms?
Red Any one: don't wait for a reply. Phone now.
Amber Vulnerability or higher need: chase sooner, and a clinician decides before closing

The tier can be raised, never lowered below what the flags show. If a flag doesn't apply, untick it and say why in the rationale.

What to do

Outcome

Messages

Reminder SMS

        
Closing SMS (no response, closing the request)

          Send this before you close a Green request, so the patient knows the request is closed and how to come back.

        
Voicemail (if you phone)

          Minimal details. Don't name the patient unless the greeting confirms it's their phone, and never leave clinical information.

        
Letter (still no response, clinical need remains)

          Be specific about why it matters. A generic "please book" letter is weaker evidence that the risk was explained.
